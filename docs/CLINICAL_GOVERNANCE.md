# Clinical content governance

MedSim content is educational and synthetic. Structural validation does not
establish medical correctness. No case, rubric, medication rule, investigation,
or source is published merely because an automated check or model accepted it.

## Publication states

`draft` → `technical-review` → `clinical-review` → `approved` →
`superseded` or `retired`.

Only `approved` versions appear to learners. Automated processes may create or
update drafts but cannot assign `approved` or `verified`.

## Required review record

- Stable content ID and semantic version.
- Intended jurisdiction, setting, and learner level.
- Learning objectives and known educational simplifications.
- Author identity and date.
- Two independent qualified clinical reviewers.
- Review decisions, comments, conflicts, and resolution.
- Approved source IDs, publication/version dates, URLs, and access dates.
- Approval date and scheduled review date.
- Content hash of the exact approved representation.

## Case checklist

- Presentation and timeline are internally coherent.
- Key positives and negatives are defined; the dialogue cannot invent either.
- One best diagnosis and four plausible distractors are justified.
- Required and unnecessary investigations are identified with rationale.
- Results include appropriate units and reference context.
- Management includes alternatives, contraindications, monitoring, follow-up,
  referral, and safety-netting where applicable.
- Demographics are relevant or deliberately varied without stereotyping.
- Assets are licensed, attributed, and clinically representative.
- Every grading criterion has observable encounter evidence.

## Source policy

Prefer current national guidance, specialty-society guidance, public-health
authorities, systematic reviews, peer-reviewed primary literature, and named
textbook editions. Search snippets, consumer pages, unsourced case banks, and
model-generated citations are not acceptable authority.

Source relevance and currency must be checked at each review. A retrievable URL
does not prove that a recommendation applies to the jurisdiction, patient, or
learner level. Superseded recommendations remain attached to historical case
versions for reproducibility but cannot be used in newly published versions.

## Validation study

For the academic release, two clinicians independently rate a stratified case
sample and learner attempts. Report agreement, disagreements, adjudication,
automated-versus-human score differences, and confidence intervals where the
sample supports them. A learner pilot measures completion, usability, perceived
feedback quality, and adverse misunderstanding. Claims in the dissertation must
not exceed the study design.

## Real data

Real patient records are prohibited unless the university has approved the
ethics, consent or waiver, de-identification, security, retention, and data-use
arrangements. Synthetic composites are the default and must never be presented
as records of actual people.
