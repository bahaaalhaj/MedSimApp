# Clinical review checklist

The reviewer must be a qualified clinician or authorized faculty member competent in the case topic and intended jurisdiction. Record reviewer ID, role, specialty, institution only with permission, review date, and relevant conflict of interest. Do not enter placeholder people.

For each case decide `accepted`, `revision-required`, `not-applicable`, or `not-reviewed` for:

- diagnosis accuracy and internal consistency of history/examination/vitals;
- appropriateness and interpretation of every investigation and image;
- plausibility and level alignment of all five diagnosis options;
- management, medicine, monitoring, contraindications, and alternatives;
- red flags, referral, escalation, and safety-netting;
- exact reference applicability, currency, section mapping, and jurisdiction;
- learner level and measurable objectives;
- rubric observability, weights, critical failures, and fairness.

Resolve every critical comment. Record an explicit approval statement and next review date. Only then may an authorized human change status to `approved-formative`. A code check, author self-review, source-link check, or AI opinion cannot approve a case.
